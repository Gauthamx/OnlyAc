## Team Section

### 3. **Hover All Member Cards** 👥

**How It Works:**
1. The team section contains 7 member cards (Gautham, Athul KR, Sidharth, Adithyan, Rahul, Goutham Ghosh, and others)
2. When you **hover your mouse over a member card**, that card is marked as "hovered"
3. The system keeps track of which cards you've hovered over
4. Once you've hovered over **ALL member cards**, the Easter egg triggers
5. A beautiful secret message appears in the center of your screen
6. The message automatically disappears after 5 seconds

**Effect:** 
- A gradient card with pink/red colors appears
- Message: *"The real treasure was the chaos we made along the way. - OnlyAc Squad"*
- Smooth animation with glowing effect
- Card appears with a scale-up animation

**Tips:**
- You need to hover ALL cards - if you miss one, you'll need to hover all of them again
- The message appears centered on screen with a beautiful gradient background
- Perfect for discovering the true meaning of the squad! 💭

## Tips for Discovery

1. **Keyboard sequences** (ONLYAC, LETSGOO, Konami code) work anywhere on the page
2. **Click-based triggers** (logo, music bars) require specific elements
3. **Hover trigger** (team cards) requires hovering all elements in sequence
4. Most effects are **temporary** - they auto-remove after a few seconds
5. **No harm** - Easter eggs won't break anything or persist annoyingly
